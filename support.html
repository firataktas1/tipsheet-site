My shift ended after midnight

Set the end time as usual. If it is earlier than the start time, Tipsheet counts it as the next morning and shows "+1". The shift stays on the day it started.

What is tip-out?

Money you hand to bussers, the bar or the kitchen at the end of the night. Enter it in the Tip-out box and Tipsheet shows the tips you actually kept.

How is overtime estimated?

By default, hours past 40 in a Monday–Sunday week count as overtime at 1.5 times the hourly wage you entered for the job. You can change the limit or turn it off in Settings. Rules differ by state and employer, so treat it as an estimate.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your shifts are stored only on your phone. Use Settings → Export all shifts to save a copy. Free cloud backup is coming in a future update.

I bought Pro on another phone

Open Pro and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.
