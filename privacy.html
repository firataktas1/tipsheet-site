Privacy Policy

Tipsheet is a tip tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. The short version: it stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Tipsheet has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking. We do not receive your shifts, tips, wages, notes or any other information you enter.

Where your data is stored

Everything you log (jobs, shifts, tips, tip-outs, wages, breaks, notes, goals and settings) is saved only in the app's storage on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Exports

When you export your shifts, the app creates a spreadsheet file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice; we never see it.

Purchases

Tipsheet Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment. We receive no payment details; the app only checks with the store whether Pro is active on your account.

Children

Tipsheet is made for working adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version starts storing data anywhere other than your phone (for example, an optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will be optional.
